Design tokens

Named values that screens use instead of raw colours and sizes, defined in tokens.css. Screens use the meaning tokens only, never the palette. The app follows the system's light or dark setting; every sample here is shown in both. Figures are worked out live from tokens.css: text needs at least 4.5:1 against its background, lines, icons, bars and chart colours at least 3:1.
